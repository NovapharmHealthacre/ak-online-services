Farmer Services

The full farmer-service scope: PM-KISAN, e-Samruddhi / eKisan Setu, PMFBY crop insurance, agricultural mechanisation subsidies and a one-stop farmer helpdesk.

Everything in this department

PM-KISAN Registration

  • New Farmer Registration — Application assistance for eligible landholding farmers.
  • Mandatory e-KYC Support — Biometric or other permitted e-KYC assistance where the centre/system is authorised for that method.
  • Land Seeding & Aadhaar Correction — Guidance correcting land-record identifiers, name spellings or Aadhaar-related mismatches.
  • Bank Account & DBT Status Updates — Verify DBT status and assist with bank-detail corrections.
  • Installment / Beneficiary Status Checks — Track application progress, installment history and pending-payment blocks.

e-Samruddhi / eKisan Setu Registration

  • New Farmer Registration — Assisted onboarding for seasonal procurement workflows.
  • Crop & Land Record Uploads — Scanning/upload support for land maps, ownership papers and crop details.
  • Aadhaar-linked e-KYC — Identity-verification guidance, including biometric method only where authorised.
  • Bank Mapping for DBT — Savings-account verification/mapping for direct-payment readiness.
  • Status Tracking & Enrollment Slip — Application tracking plus registration-slip retrieval/printing.
Seasonal procurementNAFED / NCCF linked workflowsPACS / FPO status tracking

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

  • New Crop Insurance Enrollment — Seasonal enrolment assistance for notified eligible crops.
  • Aadhaar e-KYC Verification — OTP/biometric verification guidance where the permitted method is available.
  • Land Record & Document Uploading — Land records, bank passbook and sowing-certificate scanning/upload assistance.
  • Premium Calculation & Payment Navigation — Use the applicable calculator/workflow to understand and process the farmer premium.
  • Crop Loss Intimation & Tracking — Digital assistance reporting eligible crop loss within applicable timelines and tracking the claim workflow.

Agricultural Mechanisation Subsidy

  • New Subsidy Registration — Online application assistance for approved farm implements and machinery.
  • Document Uploads — Scanning/upload of 7/12, 8-A, Aadhaar, bank passbook and quotation copies as applicable.
  • Application Tracking — Status checks from submission through departmental decision/subsidy workflow.
TractorsRotavatorsPower tillersSolar pumps where scheme permits

Farmer Helpdesk

  • Schemes & Benefits Onboarding — Registration support across PM-KISAN, PMFBY and e-Samruddhi / eKisan Setu.
  • Agricultural Mechanisation Subsidies — Application guidance for tractors, rotavators, solar pumps and power tillers where eligible.
  • e-KYC & Land Seeding — Identity-verification and land-record correction guidance to clear data mismatches/pending workflows.
  • Real-time Status Tracking — DBT payment logs, application approvals and crop-loss claim-status checks.
  • Document Vetting & Scanning — Land maps, bank passbooks and identity-proof scanning/upload support.

Typical documents to bring

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Active Mobile Number
  • Bank Passbook
  • Current Land Records — 7/12 and 8-A where applicable
  • Crop / sowing records where required
  • Quotation copies for machinery-subsidy applications where applicable
Important: Scheme eligibility, procurement acceptance, biometric authority, crop-insurance coverage/claims, subsidy sanction and DBT/payment outcomes remain with the relevant government authority, procurement agency, insurer, bank or scheme operator.
