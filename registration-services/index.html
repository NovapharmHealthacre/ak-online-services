Registration Services

Complete birth, death and marriage certificate support from the supplied file: corrections, new registrations, official digital retrieval and document alignment.

Birth Certificate

  • Birth Certificate Corrections — Correct Child's Name, Mother’s Name, Father’s Name, Date of Birth or Place of Birth where the authority permits.
  • Child Name Addition — Add the child’s official name to a certificate previously issued as Baby Boy / Baby Girl.
  • New Birth Registration Support — Digital registration assistance using hospital discharge summaries or institutional slips within the applicable process.
  • Official Digital Download & Printing — Search/retrieve and print certified digital birth records where available.

Death Certificate

  • Death Certificate Corrections — Correct Deceased’s Name, Spouse/Parents' Names, Date of Death or Place of Occurrence where permitted.
  • New Death Registration Support — Digital registration assistance using hospital/institutional records within official timelines.
  • Official Digital Download & Printing — Retrieve and print certified digital death records where available.
  • Legal Continuity Documentation Support — Help organise accurate death documentation commonly needed for bank settlement, insurance or inheritance workflows.

Marriage Certificate

  • Marriage Certificate Corrections — Correct Spouse Names, Parents' Names, Date of Marriage or Venue Details where permitted.
  • New Marriage Registration Filing — Form-filling, document upload and appointment support using wedding cards/photos/witness details under the applicable process.
  • Official Digital Download — Search, retrieve and print certified digital marriage certificates where available.
  • Document Alignment — Help align marriage documentation for downstream processes such as spouse visa, passport name change or joint bank-account requirements.

Typical documents to bring

  • Hospital documents / discharge summary for birth matters
  • Parents' Aadhaar cards for birth matters
  • Hospital summary / institutional record for death matters
  • Deceased person's Aadhaar / identity record
  • Wedding proof, photographs and invitation card where applicable
  • Bride/groom Aadhaar and witness details where applicable
Important: Civil registration, late-registration rules, correction acceptance, appointment requirements and certificate issuance remain with the relevant CRS, municipal or state authority.
