Income Tax

Local assistance with Income Tax / ITR Filing workflows, forms, supporting documents and next steps in Manjalpur, Vadodara. Call or WhatsApp before visiting to confirm current requirements.

Income Tax

  • New ITR Filing — Assistance with ITR-1, ITR-2, ITR-3 or ITR-4 selection/filing where appropriate to the taxpayer’s income profile.
  • Tax Refund Processing Support — Verification/status assistance for eligible refunds into the taxpayer’s verified bank account.
  • Aadhaar–PAN Linking — Assistance linking Aadhaar and PAN through the applicable Income Tax workflow.
  • TDS / Form 26AS / AIS / TIS Review — Retrieve and review available tax-deduction and information statements for filing preparation.
  • PAN Corrections & Linking — Support aligning PAN details where mismatches affect tax verification.
  • ITR Status & Standard Notice / e-Verification Guidance — Status checks and assisted responses/navigation for standard compliance or e-verification queries.

Before you visit

  • Call or WhatsApp to confirm the current supporting documents for this service.
  • Bring your original identity record and service-specific supporting documents where applicable.
Important: Complex tax positions, tax planning, audits, statutory certification, legal drafting, financial/investment advice, credit matching, loan approval and regulated submissions may require a CA, lawyer, company secretary, authorised lender/NBFC or other appropriately qualified/authorised professional. A K Online Services is presented as an assistance and coordination desk unless a specific professional authorisation has been independently verified.
