Digital India · Understanding e-Governance

Bringing e-Governance services closer to you

Digital India is the Government of India's programme to make government services more accessible online. Common Service Centres (CSCs) help people navigate digital services locally. A K Online Services provides practical application and document assistance in Manjalpur, Vadodara.

What is a CSC?

A human connection to digital public services.

A Common Service Centre is a local access point in India's e-Governance framework. Registered Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs) assist people with eligible online services. Each service and activity depends on the operator's applicable authorisation.
