Property & Revenue Workflows

Property & Revenue Workflows

  • Property Sales, Buying & Selling Assistance — Procedural guidance and document coordination for real-estate transactions.
  • Property Card Update — Application and record-upload support to correct eligible names, boundaries or errors on property cards.
  • Property Index Number / Index-2 Update — Assistance to retrieve or rectify eligible Index-2 / Dastavej summary records.
  • Sale Deed / Dastavej Preparation & Coordination — Digital-data and document coordination for Sale Deeds, Gift Deeds or Release Deeds before the final Sub-Registrar process.
  • Property Renting & Lease Agreements — Document assembly and registration-process support for residential/commercial rent or lease arrangements.
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC) Retrieval — Historical transaction-record search/navigation and EC application/retrieval support.
  • Property Tax Payment — Online assessment/payment navigation and official receipt support for applicable municipal/rural property tax.

Typical documents to bring

  • Current title deeds / property papers
  • Old property-tax receipts where available
  • Aadhaar / identity proof
  • Index-2 / property-card records where relevant
  • Rent/lease counterparty details where relevant
Important: Legal drafting, title opinions, conveyancing, document attestation/notarisation and any regulated property-broker activity should be undertaken by appropriately qualified or registered professionals where required. Registration and revenue decisions remain with the competent authority.
