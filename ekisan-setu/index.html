Service details

How we can help

Straightforward assistance for the main tasks within this service.

  • New Farmer Registration — Assisted onboarding for seasonal procurement workflows.
  • Crop & Land Record Uploads — Scanning/upload support for land maps, ownership papers and crop details.
  • Aadhaar-linked e-KYC — Identity-verification guidance, including biometric method only where authorised.
  • Bank Mapping for DBT — Savings-account verification/mapping for direct-payment readiness.
  • Status Tracking & Enrollment Slip — Application tracking plus registration-slip retrieval/printing.

Before you visit

  • Call or WhatsApp first so we can confirm the current documents for this exact service.
  • Bring your original identity record and service-specific supporting documents where applicable.
Important: Scheme eligibility, procurement acceptance, biometric authority, crop-insurance coverage/claims, subsidy sanction and DBT/payment outcomes remain with the relevant government authority, procurement agency, insurer, bank or scheme operator.
