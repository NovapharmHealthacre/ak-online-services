Election / Voter Card Services

End-to-end assistance across voter registration, correction, deletion/objection, physical-card requests and digital voter records.

  • New Voter Registration — Form 6 — For first-time voters aged 18+ and eligible constituency-shift cases.
  • Voter ID Updates & Corrections — Form 8 — Name, Date of Birth, Address, Photograph or Gender corrections.
  • Deletion or Objection — Form 7 — Assistance where removal from the electoral roll is required because of relocation or other eligible reasons.
  • PVC EPIC Card Printing Request — Physical PVC voter-card request support after approval by election authorities.
  • Aadhaar Linkage & e-EPIC Download — Guidance for Aadhaar linkage and digital voter-card retrieval.

Before you visit

  • Call or WhatsApp to confirm the current supporting documents for this service.
  • Bring your original identity record and service-specific supporting documents where applicable.
Important: The website presents these as assisted digital/documentation services. Official registration, verification, approval, benefit eligibility and issuance remain with the relevant authority.
