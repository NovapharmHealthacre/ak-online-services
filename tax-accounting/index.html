Income Tax

  • New ITR Filing — Assistance with ITR-1, ITR-2, ITR-3 or ITR-4 selection/filing where appropriate to the taxpayer’s income profile.
  • Tax Refund Processing Support — Verification/status assistance for eligible refunds into the taxpayer’s verified bank account.
  • Aadhaar–PAN Linking — Assistance linking Aadhaar and PAN through the applicable Income Tax workflow.
  • TDS / Form 26AS / AIS / TIS Review — Retrieve and review available tax-deduction and information statements for filing preparation.
  • PAN Corrections & Linking — Support aligning PAN details where mismatches affect tax verification.
  • ITR Status & Standard Notice / e-Verification Guidance — Status checks and assisted responses/navigation for standard compliance or e-verification queries.

GST

  • New GST Registration — GSTIN application assistance with document preparation and portal navigation.
  • GST Return Filing — Assistance preparing/submitting applicable GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-4 (Composition) returns.
  • Input Tax Credit Reconciliation — Purchase-invoice review against GSTR-2B to support eligible ITC reconciliation.
  • GST Nil Return Filing — Nil-return assistance for applicable periods/businesses.
  • Profile Corrections & Core Amendments — Business Name, Additional Place of Business, Mobile Number and Authorised Signatory updates where permitted.
  • E-Way Bill Generation — Digital e-Way Bill generation support where required under the applicable goods-movement rules.

Accounting

  • Daily Bookkeeping & Ledger Maintenance — Sales, purchases, expenses, vouchers and cash-flow records.
  • Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet Preparation — Preparation support for business financial statements based on supplied accounting records.
  • Project Report & Loan Documentation — Project-report and financial-document preparation support for Mudra/commercial loan applications.
  • Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS) — Match internal books against bank records to identify missing or inconsistent entries.
  • Inventory & Stock Management — Basic digital stock tracking for shop/warehouse inventory.
  • Financial Alignment for Taxes — Organise billing/accounting data for GST and annual Income Tax preparation.

Business Loan & Personal Loan Assistance

  • Mudra & MSME Business Loans — Application-document assistance for applicable PMMY categories and MSME credit products.
  • Personal Loans — Profile/document preparation assistance for salaried or self-employed applicants.
  • Digital Application & Lead Generation — Assisted submission/lead-generation where an authorised bank/NBFC/CSC workflow is available.
  • CIBIL Score Check Guidance — Basic guidance obtaining/understanding credit-score information before an application.
  • Document Assembly & Project Reports — Aadhaar, PAN, bank statements, ITR, Udyam/business records and project-report assembly.
  • DBT & Subsidy Mapping — Guided checks for applicable central/state interest-subsidy or business-support schemes.

Registrar of Companies (ROC) Compliance

  • New Company & LLP Incorporation — Documentation and online-filing assistance for SPICe+ / incorporation workflows.
  • Annual ROC Return Filing — Preparation/submission support for applicable MGT-7/MGT-7A and AOC-4 filings.
  • DIN & DIR-3 KYC — DIN allotment and annual KYC workflow assistance where applicable.
  • Company Profile Updates & Amendments — Registered-office change, share-capital changes and director appointment/resignation filing support where applicable.
  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) Assistance — Acquisition/renewal workflow assistance for Class-3 DSC where required.
  • Master Data & Document Retrieval — Retrieve/print available company master data and public registry documents.

Company Creation

  • Private Limited Company Incorporation — Digital setup assistance for a Private Limited Company.
  • LLP / OPC Formation — Formation assistance based on the selected entity structure.
  • SPICe+ Filing — Unified incorporation-form preparation/submission assistance.
  • DIN & Name Approval — Director-identification and company-name workflow assistance.
  • DSC Issuance / Renewal Workflow — Class-3 DSC application/renewal assistance where required.
  • MOA & AOA Drafting Coordination — Structural/document coordination for MOA/AOA; legal drafting should be handled by the appropriately qualified professional where required.

Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)

  • HUF Deed / Declaration Assistance — Document-structure assistance covering Karta, Coparceners and family members.
  • New HUF PAN Application — Digital PAN application assistance for the HUF entity.
  • HUF Bank Account Documentation — Assemble identity/deed documentation commonly requested by banks.
  • Tax Identification & Alignment — Organise tax-identification records for HUF-related income/property/business records.
  • Document Vetting & Scanning — Karta identity proofs, family details and signed declaration scanning/upload support.

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

  • LLP Incorporation — FiLLiP — Digital filing support for LLP incorporation.
  • Name Approval — RUN-LLP — Name-reservation workflow assistance.
  • LLP Agreement Coordination — Structural/document coordination for partner roles, profit sharing and capital terms.
  • PAN & TAN Allotment Workflow — Assistance with the PAN/TAN steps associated with LLP incorporation.
  • DSC Assistance — Class-3 DSC application/renewal workflow support for designated partners where required.
  • Partner Document Vetting & Scanning — Aadhaar, PAN, bank statements and office utility-bill preparation/upload support.

Partnership Firm

  • Partnership Deed Coordination — Document-structure coordination for capital contribution, profit sharing, roles and dispute-resolution terms.
  • New Partnership PAN Application — Digital PAN application assistance for the partnership firm.
  • Registrar of Firms (RoF) Registration — Compilation/formatting/submission assistance for applicable state RoF registration.
  • Current Bank Account Documentation — Assemble partnership deed, firm PAN and business-address proofs commonly requested by banks.
  • Stamp Duty & Judicial e-Challan — Online payment/process assistance for applicable stamp-duty requirements.
  • Partner Document Vetting & Scanning — Aadhaar, PAN, passport photos and office utility-bill scanning/upload support.

Typical documents to bring

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Bank Passbook / bank statements
  • Form 16 / income details for Income Tax work
  • Business PAN, GST records and invoices for GST/accounting work
  • Rent agreement / property proof for business registrations where applicable
  • Financial statements / purchase bills / daily sales records for accounting work
  • Director / partner IDs and incorporation records for ROC/company work
  • Last six months bank statements and income/business proofs for loan applications where requested
Important: Complex tax positions, tax planning, audits, statutory certification, legal drafting, financial/investment advice, credit matching, loan approval and regulated submissions may require a CA, lawyer, company secretary, authorised lender/NBFC or other appropriately qualified/authorised professional. A K Online Services is presented as an assistance and coordination desk unless a specific professional authorisation has been independently verified.
