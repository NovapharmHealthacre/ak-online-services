Tax, Accounting & Financial Services

A broad business-finance desk covering Income Tax, GST, bookkeeping/accounting, loan paperwork and corporate registration/compliance support.

☎ Call the deskWhatsApp

Income Tax / ITR

Assistance with appropriate ITR filing, refund status, PAN-Aadhaar linkage, Form 26AS/AIS/TIS review, PAN corrections and standard status/e-verification queries.

GST Services

New GST registration, applicable return-filing support, ITC reconciliation assistance, Nil returns, profile amendments and e-Way Bill generation support.

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Daily bookkeeping, ledger maintenance, P&L and balance-sheet preparation support, bank reconciliation, inventory tracking and tax-ready financial organisation.

Business & Personal Loan Paperwork

Mudra/MSME and personal-loan application assistance, document assembly, CIBIL guidance, project reports and subsidy/DBT mapping support.

ROC Compliance

Company/LLP incorporation assistance, annual ROC filing support, DIN/KYC, company-profile amendments, DSC assistance and registry-document retrieval.

Company Creation

Private Limited, OPC and LLP setup support including SPICe+, name approval, DIN, DSC and MOA/AOA coordination.

HUF Services

HUF deed/declaration support, HUF PAN application, bank-account documentation and tax-document alignment.

LLP Services

FiLLiP / RUN-LLP support, LLP Agreement coordination, PAN/TAN, DSC and partner-document preparation.

Partnership Firm Services

Partnership-deed coordination, firm PAN, Registrar of Firms filing, bank-account documentation and stamp-duty/e-challan support.

Typical documents

Requirements vary widely. They may include PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements/passbooks, Form 16/income details, GST/business records, rent/property proof, financial statements, director/partner IDs and incorporation records.

Important: Complex tax positions, audits, statutory certification, legal drafting, regulated financial advice and lending decisions must remain with appropriately qualified professionals, authorities, banks or NBFCs. A K Online Services is presented as an assistance/coordination desk unless a specific professional authorisation is independently verified.
