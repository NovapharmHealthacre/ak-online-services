Passport Application Workflows

Passport Application Workflows

  • New Passport Application — Step-by-step assistance for first-time Indian passport applicants.
  • Reissue of Passport — Assistance where a passport is expiring/expired, pages are exhausted or reissue is otherwise required.
  • Lost or Damaged Passport Replacement — Application and document-preparation support for misplaced, stolen or physically damaged passports.
  • Name Change in Passport — Eligible name-change assistance after marriage, divorce or an official Gazette notification.
  • Address Change in Passport — Update current permanent/residential address in the passport application workflow.
  • Tatkaal Passport Assistance — Application and urgent-appointment guidance for eligible Tatkaal cases.
  • Appointment Scheduling & Fee Payment — Government-fee payment navigation and PSK/POPSK appointment-booking support.

Typical documents to bring

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Birth proof
  • Current address proof
  • Existing passport for reissue where applicable
  • Police/loss documentation where required for lost/stolen cases
  • Marriage / divorce / Gazette records where applicable to a name change
Important: A K Online Services is not a Passport Seva Kendra. Passport verification, police verification, eligibility, approval, appointments controlled by the portal and issuance remain with Passport Seva / Ministry of External Affairs and competent authorities.
