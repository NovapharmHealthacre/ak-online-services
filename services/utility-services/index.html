Utility Services

The full utility scope from the supplied file: MGVCL electricity, Vadodara Gas Limited PNG and LPG services including connections, name transfers, billing, refill booking and subsidy-status support.

Everything in this department

All three utility families and every task listed in the source are represented below, including the specific consumer identifiers/documents customers are asked to bring.

MGVCL Services

  • New MGVCL Connection Application — Digital application and document-upload assistance for eligible domestic, commercial or agricultural connections.
  • Name Update & Transfer — Consumer-name change/transfer support after eligible property purchase, inheritance or marriage cases.
  • Billing & Bill Payment — Outstanding-bill retrieval, electronic payment assistance and receipt support.

Vadodara Gas Limited / PNG Services

  • New PNG Connection Application — Digital application and document-upload assistance for eligible domestic or commercial piped-gas connections.
  • Name Update & Transfer — Consumer-name update/transfer support after eligible property purchase, inheritance or marriage cases.
  • Billing & Bill Payment — Outstanding PNG bill retrieval, electronic payment assistance and receipt support.

LPG Cylinder Services

  • New LPG Connection Application — Fresh domestic LPG connection assistance, including document support for eligible subsidy schemes such as PMUY.
  • Name Update & Transfer — Consumer-name change/transfer support after marriage, property shift or eligible legal-name change.
  • Refill Booking, Billing & Subsidy Status — Digital refill booking, payment/receipt assistance and LPG subsidy-credit status guidance.

Typical documents to bring

  • MGVCL Consumer Number / Consumer ID
  • Current identity proof
  • Property papers such as 7/12 or Index-2 where applicable
  • VGL BP Number for PNG matters
  • LPG Consumer ID
  • Gas Passbook
  • Aadhaar Card
Important: Connection approval, meter/installation scheduling, official billing, subsidy eligibility, service activation and utility-provider decisions remain with MGVCL, Vadodara Gas Limited or the relevant LPG provider/scheme authority.
