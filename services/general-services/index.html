PAN Card Services

Assistance for new PAN applications, corrections, Aadhaar-linked verification and replacement cards.

  • New PAN Card Application — Application assistance for both individuals and businesses with document guidance.
  • PAN Card Correction & Updates — Name, Date of Birth, Father’s Name, Photo or Signature corrections so records align with supporting documents.
  • OTP e-KYC — Aadhaar-linked OTP based paperless verification support.
  • Physical Card Reprinting — Replacement request support for lost, damaged or faded physical PAN cards.

Election / Voter Card Services

End-to-end assistance across voter registration, correction, deletion/objection, physical-card requests and digital voter records.

  • New Voter Registration — Form 6 — For first-time voters aged 18+ and eligible constituency-shift cases.
  • Voter ID Updates & Corrections — Form 8 — Name, Date of Birth, Address, Photograph or Gender corrections.
  • Deletion or Objection — Form 7 — Assistance where removal from the electoral roll is required because of relocation or other eligible reasons.
  • PVC EPIC Card Printing Request — Physical PVC voter-card request support after approval by election authorities.
  • Aadhaar Linkage & e-EPIC Download — Guidance for Aadhaar linkage and digital voter-card retrieval.

Ration Card Services

  • New Ration Card Registration — Assisted filing for eligible families applying for a fresh ration card.
  • Member Updates & Corrections — Add family members, delete names, or correct names, addresses and relationship details.
  • Status Checks & Grievance Registration — Check ration availability/application status and register complaints about ration delivery.

e-Shram Services

  • Profile Updates & Corrections — Mobile Number, Current Address, Occupation Type, Educational Qualifications and Income Details.
  • Bank Account Update & Seeding — Bank Account Number and IFSC correction/update for DBT readiness.
  • New e-Shram Registration — Onboarding support for eligible unorganised-sector workers such as labourers, street vendors, domestic helpers and farmers.
  • Biometric & OTP Authentication — Aadhaar-linked OTP or fingerprint-authentication guidance where the applicable system permits it.
  • UAN Card Printing — Physical print support for the e-Shram Universal Account Number card.

Udyam / MSME Registration

  • Udyam Certificate Updates & Corrections — Business Name, Mobile Number, Plant/Office Address, Bank Details, Investment or Turnover updates.
  • New Udyam MSME Registration — Step-by-step assistance for eligible micro, small and medium businesses.
  • NIC Code Corrections — Assistance aligning business activities to the appropriate National Industrial Classification codes.
  • PAN & GSTIN Integration — Assistance linking/verifying business PAN and GST details on the Udyam portal.
  • Certificate Retrieval & Printing — Retrieve, download and print the Udyam certificate.

Gazette Notification Assistance

  • Name Change Gazette Assistance — For eligible name changes after marriage, adoption, astrological reasons or major spelling mismatches.
  • Religion & Gender Change Notifications — Application preparation guidance for official record-change notifications.
  • Affidavit & Document Verification — Guidance preparing affidavits, newspaper advertisement text and identity proofs before submission.
  • Digital Application Filing — Scanning, uploading and payment-process assistance on the applicable publication workflow.
  • Digital Gazette Download & Printing — Application-status tracking and download/print support once the Gazette is published.

Typical documents to bring

  • Aadhaar / identity proof as applicable
  • Bank passbook for e-Shram / Udyam-related banking details
  • Business PAN and bank details for Udyam
  • Existing card / registration details for corrections
  • Current ID and name-change documents for Gazette work
Important: The website presents these as assisted digital/documentation services. Official registration, verification, approval, benefit eligibility and issuance remain with the relevant authority.
