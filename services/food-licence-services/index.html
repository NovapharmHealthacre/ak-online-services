Food Licence / FSSAI Services

The complete FSSAI workflow supplied for this department: new registration, State/Central licence support, renewal, modifications, document vetting/upload and certificate/status support.

☎ Call the deskWhatsApp

Everything in this department

All six tasks from the Food Licence document are preserved below. Turnover/category thresholds should always be applied using the current FSSAI rules in force at the time of filing.

FSSAI Registration & Licensing

  • New FSSAI Registration (Form A) — Application assistance for eligible small food businesses, hawkers, petty retailers and start-ups under the applicable FSSAI category.
  • State & Central FSSAI Licence Filing (Form B) — Guided application, document assembly and fee-process support for businesses falling into the applicable State or Central licence category.
  • FSSAI Licence Renewal — Renewal assistance before an existing registration/licence expires.
  • Information Modification & Updates — Business Name, Premise Address, Food Product Categories and Contact Details corrections/updates.
  • Document Vetting & Uploading — Scanning/upload support for Aadhaar, Business PAN, electricity bills, rent agreements, layout plans and other applicable records.
  • Certificate Download & Status Tracking — Track application progress and retrieve/print the FSSAI registration/licence certificate after approval.

Typical documents to bring

  • Identity proof / Aadhaar
  • Business PAN where applicable
  • Business address proof
  • Electricity bill
  • Rent agreement where applicable
  • Layout plan where required
  • Passport-sized photographs where required
Important: FSSAI category/turnover thresholds, registration or licence eligibility, scrutiny, approval and certificate issuance remain with the competent food-safety authority and current FoSCoS rules.
