Certificate Services

Detailed assistance for Income, Domicile, Caste / Non-Creamy Layer and EWS certificates, including filing, document vetting, declarations, tracking and printing.

☎ Call the deskWhatsApp

Everything in this department

The four certificate workflows from the supplied Certification Agency document are fully separated below with their individual filing and document requirements.

Income Certificate (Aavak no Dakhlo)

  • New Application Filing — Digital form filling aligned to the applicable local revenue-authority workflow.
  • Document Vetting & Uploading — Salary slips, ITR copies, employer certificates or self-declarations as applicable.
  • Affidavit & Verification Support — Guidance organising required declarations and identity proofs.
  • Digital Tracking & Printing — Track status and print the digitally signed certificate after approval.
School / college admissionsScholarshipsWelfare schemes

Domicile Certificate

  • New Domicile Registration — Digital application assistance under the applicable state revenue process.
  • Residency Proof Vetting & Scanning — Continuous-residency proofs such as school leaving certificates, electricity bills or voting records.
  • Affidavit & Verification Assistance — Guidance arranging local declarations and identity verification.
  • Digital Tracking & Printing — Track application status and print the certificate after approval.
College admissionsGovernment jobsState welfare schemes

Caste Certificate & Non-Creamy Layer

  • New Caste Application Filing — Digital application support for eligible SC, ST and OBC/SEBC applicants.
  • Document Vetting & Scanning — Pedigree charts (Panshaoli), school leaving certificates of self/father and old family caste records as applicable.
  • Non-Creamy Layer Assistance — Assistance for eligible OBC/SEBC applicants requiring NCL documentation.
  • Digital Tracking & Printing — Track the application and print the digitally signed certificate after approval.
ReservationsAdmissionsScholarshipsGovernment employment

Economically Weaker Section (EWS) Certificate

  • New EWS Application Filing — Digital form-filling assistance under the applicable financial-eligibility workflow.
  • Income & Asset Document Vetting — Family income details, agricultural land holdings and residential-property documents as applicable.
  • Affidavit & Declaration Vetting — Guidance organising self-declarations and family-income documentation.
  • Status Tracking & Printing — Track the application and print the digitally signed EWS certificate after approval.
Higher educationGovernment recruitmentEWS reservation workflows

Typical documents to bring

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Ration Card where required
  • Electricity bill / residence proof
  • Income proof / ITR / employer certificate as applicable
  • School Leaving Certificate / family caste proof for caste matters
  • PAN Card and land/property documents for EWS where applicable
  • Passport-sized photographs where required
Important: Applications can be assisted here, but certificate eligibility, verification, approval and issuance remain with the competent government/revenue authority.
